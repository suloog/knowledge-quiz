# Age-suitability check — 5+ deck (US-01-04)

The check every question of `src/content/decks/5-plus.json` passed before it was accepted (`MOD-01-F06`, `MOD-01-F14`, `Q29`, `DEC-26`, `DEC-46`, `DEC-47`). Made in the delivery loop on 2026-10-15 against both languages; each row shows the question and its answer in Polish and in English, and each check holds for both. The product owner reviews all content once, before release (`DEC-26`).

The 5+ deck is the hardest of the three (`MOD-01-F06`, `DEC-51`): numbers past ten, the months, hours and minutes and the clock, the planets and the body, the sides of the world, how nature works, record-holding animals, and Poland and the town around us. Every question is an open question: none names answer options (`DEC-47`). No question repeats a 3+ or 4+ question, in its text or in what it asks for (`US-01-04#6`, `DEC-50`, `DEC-51`).

The four checks, each `pass` or `fail`:

- **Known words** — every word is one a child of 5 knows, or one a 5-year-old meets in picture books and in the last year of preschool (planeta, szkielet, kompas, roślinożerca, godło).
- **Nothing frightening** — nothing frightening, violent or adult.
- **One answer** — the question has one unambiguous answer, the one given, in each language: neither a child nor a parent reading it aloud can give another answer that is also correct. Each question was attacked in both languages for such an answer — another thing that fits it, a near miss — by the implementer and by an independent reviewer, and reworded until none was left: a distinguishing detail, a more specific question, or another fact. A synonym naming the same thing (an author for a writer) is the same answer. The **Near misses checked** column names, for every question, the answers tried against it and why each is ruled out.
- **Verifiable** — the answer is a fact anyone can check.

A question is accepted only when all four are `pass`; `tests/unit/AC-01-04-4-age-suitability-record.test.js` fails on any question of the deck without such a row, on a row that names no near miss, and on any 5+ question that offers options.
