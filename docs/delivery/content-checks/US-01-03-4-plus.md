# Age-suitability check — 4+ deck (US-01-03)

The check every question of `src/content/decks/4-plus.json` passed before it was accepted (`MOD-01-F06`, `MOD-01-F14`, `Q29`, `DEC-26`, `DEC-46`, `DEC-47`). Made in the delivery loop on 2026-10-15 against both languages; each row shows the question and its answer in Polish and in English, and each check holds for both. The product owner reviews all content once, before release (`DEC-26`).

The 4+ deck asks a little more than 3+ (`MOD-01-F06`): counting to ten, the days of the week, shapes, mixing colours, opposites, animal babies and how animals live, what things are made of and how they change, and the people who help us. Every question is an open question: none names answer options (`DEC-47`). No question repeats a 3+ question, in its text or in what it asks for (`US-01-03#6`).

The four checks, each `pass` or `fail`:

- **Known words** — every word is one a child of 4 knows, or one a 4-year-old meets in picture books and at preschool (gawra, żołądź, lupa).
- **Nothing frightening** — nothing frightening, violent or adult.
- **One answer** — the question has one unambiguous answer, the one given, in each language: neither a child nor a parent reading it aloud can give another answer that is also correct. Each question was attacked in both languages for such an answer — another thing that fits it, a near miss — and reworded until none was left: a distinguishing detail, a more specific question, or another fact. A synonym naming the same thing (a zipper for a zip) is the same answer. The **Near misses checked** column names, for every question, the answers tried against it and why each is ruled out.
- **Verifiable** — the answer is a fact anyone can check.

A question is accepted only when all four are `pass`; `tests/unit/AC-01-03-4-age-suitability-record.test.js` fails on any question of the deck without such a row, on a row that names no near miss, and on any 4+ question that offers options.
